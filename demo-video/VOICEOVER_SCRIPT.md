# Atlas demo voiceover (English)

Atlas is a SocialFi growth layer for protocols on Portaldot.

Protocols create campaigns with clear rules, proof methods and reward boundaries. Communities discover a route, complete a quest and submit the evidence requested by that campaign.

Atlas turns each meaningful action into reviewable progress. Operators can inspect the proof, record a decision and sign the reward only after the contribution is accepted.

Once approved, GemLedger records the reward on-chain and the participant can verify the result. That creates a repeatable path from discovery to participation, verification and reward — while giving protocols a practical layer for acquisition, engagement and retention.

Atlas is built to help ecosystems grow through campaigns people can understand, complete and come back to.

## Recording direction

- Target length: 55–70 seconds for the teaser cut, or 90–120 seconds for the full cut.
- Read calmly and leave a short pause between each paragraph.
- Keep product terms in English: Campaign Studio, Campaign Board, Review Queue, proof, Gems, GemLedger.
- Do not mention local demos, testnet limitations, missing integrations or internal setup.
