# Atlas submission voiceover (English)

Atlas is a SocialFi growth layer for protocols on Portaldot.

Protocols create campaigns with clear rules, proof methods and reward boundaries. Campaign Studio gives the protocol team one place to define the route, set its budget and decide what a meaningful contribution looks like.

On the participant side, Atlas turns that brief into a clear Campaign Board journey. A user discovers the campaign, completes the quest and submits the requested proof together with the wallet that should receive the reward.

The proof does not become a reward automatically. It enters a shared review queue where an operator can inspect the evidence, record a decision and keep an auditable history of the campaign state. This creates a real boundary between participation, verification and issuance.

Once the contribution is accepted, the operator signs one controlled grant on Portaldot V3. GemLedger records the reward on-chain, and the participant can verify the result from the product flow and the public explorer. Gems represent campaign progress and give the community a reason to return to the next route.

This architecture is intentionally protocol-ready. The first environment is Portaldot, while future adapters can add campaign analytics, boosters, collectible modules and network-specific reward paths without changing the core participant journey.

Atlas connects acquisition, participation, reviewable contribution and retention in one repeatable loop: discover, complete, review and reward.
